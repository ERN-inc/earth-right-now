## Before posting

1. Check for an existing listing to avoid duplicate Product Hunt submissions. Product Hunt may require a relaunch request for a recent same-link launch.
2. Capture a genuine current screenshot and ensure rights to every submitted image; do not create a misleading satellite-camera depiction.
3. Confirm the stream works, site loads, and any claimed 24/7 availability matches reality.
4. Use the platform's own authenticated submission process with a real authorized account. Do not invent an email or claim a submission.
5. If accepted, record the public listing URL, date and GA4 referral acquisition, distinguishing impressions, referral clicks and actual website visits.
6. Do not buy priority review, paid promotion, artificial engagement or upvotes.
