## Recommended first educational activity

**Title:** Why is it sunrise in one city and sunset in another?

**Audience:** Geography / Earth science students, approximately ages 11–16.

**Learning goals:** (1) explain Earth's rotation and longitude, (2) distinguish longitude effects from latitude/season effects, (3) distinguish a visual model from measured satellite or astronomical data.

**Class activity:** Students compare Tokyo, Jakarta, Reykjavík and Sydney using ERN city guides, predict which regions are experiencing morning versus evening, then check an authoritative astronomical source for exact times and discuss differences. Never claim ERN visualization provides precise observed times.
