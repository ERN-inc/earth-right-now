Turn your browser into a live Earth display

EARTH RIGHT NOW is a free browser-based Earth visualization with a live video stream. You can leave it open on a spare monitor, living-room display, cafe screen or home screen to enjoy changing day-and-night scenes without installing a desktop screensaver.

Open the live player, start playback if your browser requires a click, and choose full-screen mode. Keep the device connected to the internet. On laptops and phones, long playback sessions can use substantial battery and data, so a power connection and Wi-Fi are useful.

A browser display is not an operating-system screensaver. It does not automatically lock your computer or protect your screen from static elements. Use your device's own lock and display-sleep settings for privacy and screen care.

Ideas for relaxing ambient Earth displays

Work-from-home backdrop: Keep the live Earth view on a second monitor while working. Relaxing evening viewing: Watch city moments and the day–night rhythm on a larger screen. Cafes, hotels and travel spaces: Consider a quiet world-themed visual background, subject to your display and YouTube usage rights. City discovery: Explore Tokyo, London, Jakarta and Reykjavík as the world changes.

Can I use it as a real screensaver?

ERN runs in a browser tab and can be viewed full-screen, but it does not replace your computer’s built-in screen lock or automatic screensaver. For an always-on display, check your device power settings and your network connection. For OLED displays, avoid leaving fixed interface elements visible for very long periods.

Website owners who want to feature the live Earth view can use the responsive embedding instructions; playback depends on the current YouTube broadcast and applicable platform terms.

If video playback stops, check your network, browser autoplay permissions and the official YouTube live channel. The broadcast can be temporarily unavailable.
