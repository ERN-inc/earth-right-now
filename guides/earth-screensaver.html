Turn your browser into a live Earth display

EARTH RIGHT NOW is a free browser-based Earth visualization with a live video stream. You can leave it open on a spare monitor, classroom display or home screen to enjoy changing day-and-night scenes without installing a desktop screensaver.

Open the live player, start playback if your browser requires a click, and choose full-screen mode. Keep the device connected to the internet. On laptops and phones, long playback sessions can use substantial battery and data, so a power connection and Wi-Fi are useful.

A browser display is not an operating-system screensaver. It does not automatically lock your computer or protect your screen from static elements. Use your device's own lock and display-sleep settings for privacy and screen care.

If video playback stops, check your network, browser autoplay permissions and the official YouTube live channel. The broadcast can be temporarily unavailable.
