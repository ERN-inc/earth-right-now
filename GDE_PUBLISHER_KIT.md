# EARTH RIGHT NOW — Organic Publisher & Community Sharing Kit

**Goal:** Help interested educators, science communicators, geography clubs and website editors discover the free ERN experience and send genuine visitors to the official site. This is a resource for voluntary, relevant sharing—not an automated outreach list.

**Official destination:** https://ern-inc.github.io/earth-right-now/

**Important accuracy:** ERN is a live broadcast of an Earth visualization covering sunrise, sunset and city moments across 286 cities. It is not a real-time satellite camera or a certified astronomical/weather source. Stream availability may vary.

## Responsible distribution checklist

1. Share only where the content directly answers a community's question or meets an editor's stated submission policy.
2. Prefer a useful explanation and a relevant deep link over a generic promotional message.
3. Do not send unsolicited bulk messages, post repeated links, buy traffic, fabricate views, or imply third-party endorsement.
4. Do not claim ERN provides exact live satellite, weather, or astronomical measurements.
5. Check links and current stream status before submitting. Respect moderation and rate limits.
6. When allowed, record the channel, date, approved public placement URL and referral traffic for later assessment. Do not claim an external placement until it is actually published.
