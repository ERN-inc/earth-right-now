# ERN GLOBAL DISTRIBUTION ENGINE (GDE) — ROADMAP

Start date: 2026-11-14
Goal: Build a zero-extra-cost, policy-compliant, fully automated global distribution system for EARTH RIGHT NOW (ERN).

## Non-negotiable rules
- Do not modify the ERN desktop application.
- Zero additional paid services or paid APIs unless explicitly approved later.
- No spam, unsolicited bulk messaging, deceptive redirects, forced browsing, fake engagement, bot traffic, or platform-rule evasion.
- Do not call unfinished or unverified work complete.
- Prefer reversible, low-risk changes.
- Preserve the existing ERN website visual design unless a change is required for distribution functionality.
- Keep all automation auditable in GitHub.

## President-approved funnel and change control (2026-11-15)
**Primary acquisition destination: the ERN official website.** Attract real worldwide visitors to https://ern-inc.github.io/earth-right-now/ first; from the official site, let visitors watch the embedded YouTube live stream or follow the YouTube link. The direction is **external discovery → ERN official website → YouTube viewing**. Do not replace this with a YouTube-first funnel or prioritize YouTube channel traffic as the principal KPI. Keep the existing embedded live player and YouTube link; no desktop-app changes.

**Decision authority:** Before starting a new acquisition channel, changing the audience, launching a campaign or altering the funnel, present the specific action, expected benefit, cost and risks to the President and obtain approval. A general instruction to continue does not authorize strategic redirection. Routine implementation within an explicitly approved scope may proceed, with verifiable reporting. Prioritize real website sessions and Google Search clicks/impressions; commits, tests and deployments are supporting evidence only.

ERN is a relaxing, captivating, always-on Earth viewing experience first—not an education product. Prioritize genuine visits from (1) live-stream viewers, (2) ambient/relaxation and work-background audiences, (3) screensaver and visual display searches, (4) travel/city/landscape fans, and (5) hotels, cafes and display publishers. Educational directory outreach and classroom-resource production are paused. Existing accurate explanatory pages may remain for organic discovery, but do not prioritize educational pitching. The previously researched education prospects are archived research only, not an active outreach queue. Optimize actual website referrals and repeat human visits, not commits or speculative impressions.

## Priority acquisition experiment — EARTH BREAK short-form discovery (2026-11-22)

**Strategic hypothesis, not a proven outcome:** ERN's strongest zero-extra-cost growth path is a repeatable, genuinely distinctive 10–30 second clip of actual ERN sunrise/sunset/day-night/city moments, linked to the official ERN website. Use the existing YouTube and Pinterest presences first; adding a new channel or launching an external campaign still requires specific approval under the President-approved change control.

**Why:** Existing Pinterest board has one Pin and a screenshot showed 12 impressions, 7 Pin interactions, and zero saves; those 7 are not necessarily outbound website clicks. Pinterest's own guidance says original high-quality images/videos, fresh weekly publication, relevant destinations and titles, and distinct creative variants matter; duplicate posting risks spam flags. YouTube has expanded Shorts-from-clips tooling for eligible long-form content, but feature eligibility for ERN must be verified. Search visibility depends on helpful original content, not mass-produced thin pages.

**Execution sequence (do not substitute document commits for delivery):**
1. Verify public website, existing live stream, and access to actual ERN-owned footage before preparing clips. Never invent footage or represent visualization as satellite camera footage.
2. Produce 3 distinct, source-verified vertical clips (10–30 seconds): (a) sunrise crossing Earth, (b) nightfall, (c) 286-city moment. Caption simply, with EARTH BREAK tone. Source footage and commercial rights must be verified.
3. Use official-site-first tracked destination URL for Pinterest: `https://ern-inc.github.io/earth-right-now/?utm_source=pinterest&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=earth_break`. For YouTube, respect platform link capabilities; use the official site in profile/description where permitted. Avoid promises of clickable Shorts links.
4. On the existing approved channels, obtain publication approval for the exact clips and scheduling if needed; do not assume this document authorizes a new campaign or connected-account access.
5. Track separately: Pinterest impressions, saves, **outbound clicks**, GA4 sessions by UTM source, Google Search Console clicks and impressions, and returning visitors. The 7 interactions shown in screenshot are not proof of 7 site visits.
6. Compare 3 creative concepts after a defined initial observation period (e.g. 7 days), then scale the winner with **new original footage**, not duplicate reposts. Stop low-value tactics.
7. Separately, maintain the existing voluntary embed program, but do not divert the main growth effort into researching tiny directories.

**P0 blockers:** confirmed access to source clip footage; channel publication credentials/connection; real GA4/Search Console acquisition data. Status as of 2026-11-22: strategy selected, no new clip published or traffic lift verified by this roadmap entry.

## Daily execution rule
Each daily automation run must:
1. Read this roadmap and current repository state.
2. Determine the current phase by date and incomplete exit criteria.
3. Inspect latest GitHub Actions and public distribution files.
4. Execute as many safe, reversible, zero-cost tasks as can be completed and verified in the run without requiring new user credentials or consent; do not artificially limit the run to one task.
5. Verify the change.
6. Report exactly what changed, what was verified, and what remains.
7. If blocked by a credential/permission/manual platform action, report the smallest required user action instead of guessing.

## Weekly audit rule
Once per week:
- Compare progress against exit criteria.
- Check failures, stale metadata, broken public links, duplicate/low-quality SEO risks and automation drift.
- Reprioritize the next seven days.
- Never inflate completion status.
