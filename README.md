# EARTH RIGHT NOW (ERN) — Live Earth, Sunrise & Sunset Around the World

**A free live Earth visualization for relaxing, ambient viewing, work backgrounds and discovering 286 cities around the world.**

🌍 **[Watch EARTH RIGHT NOW](https://ern-inc.github.io/earth-right-now/)** · [YouTube live channel](https://www.youtube.com/@earth_right_now/live) · [World city guides](https://ern-inc.github.io/earth-right-now/cities/)

ERN is a live broadcast of an Earth visualization featuring sunrise, sunset and city moments. It is **not** a real-time satellite camera or an authoritative source for precise astronomical or weather observations. Broadcast availability can vary.

## Relax, watch and explore the planet

Open the [free live Earth view](https://ern-inc.github.io/earth-right-now/) on a desktop screen or browser tab as an ambient background. Enjoy the changing day–night rhythm, city moments and a visual journey around the planet. Looking for a browser-based display? See the [free Earth screensaver guide](https://ern-inc.github.io/earth-right-now/guides/earth-screensaver.html).
