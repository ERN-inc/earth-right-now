ABOUT THE PROJECT

EARTH RIGHT NOW

EARTH RIGHT NOW (ERN) is a continuously running visual experience inspired by the living planet. The broadcast combines a global Earth view with changing daylight and location-based moments such as sunrise and sunset.

It is a visual simulation and broadcast, not a live satellite camera feed. Events, imagery, and information shown in the stream may vary over time.

Make Earth your background

Open the live player, select Full Screen, and enjoy the Earth view on your monitor. For uninterrupted viewing, keep your device awake and your internet connection active.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is EARTH RIGHT NOW free?

Yes. You can watch the embedded live stream on this website for free.

Is this a real-time satellite camera?

No. ERN is an Earth visualization and live broadcast experience, not a direct satellite camera feed.

Can I use it as a desktop screensaver?

You can use the full-screen browser player as a screensaver-like display. It does not install or replace your operating system's screensaver.

Why is the video not playing?

Check your internet connection and browser settings, or use the Watch on YouTube button. The live stream may also be temporarily unavailable.
