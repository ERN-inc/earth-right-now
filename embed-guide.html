Embed EARTH RIGHT NOW on your website

EARTH RIGHT NOW (ERN) presents a 24/7 live Earth experience with sunrise, sunset and city moments across 286 cities. Publishers, educators and community sites can add the live player without copying video files or maintaining a YouTube video ID.

Copy the responsive embed

<iframe src="https://ern-inc.github.io/earth-right-now/embed.html" title="EARTH RIGHT NOW live Earth stream" loading="lazy" allow="autoplay; encrypted-media; picture-in-picture; fullscreen" allowfullscreen style="width:100%;aspect-ratio:16/9;border:0"></iframe>

Use a container with a suitable width. The player reads ERN's public live status and selects the current YouTube broadcast. When no live stream is available, the embed redirects to ERN's YouTube live channel. Some browsers may restrict autoplay or embedded playback.

Attribution and accessibility

Suggested attribution: EARTH RIGHT NOW — Live Earth. Keep a descriptive iframe title and provide a direct link to the source for users who cannot access embedded video.

Where the live information comes from

The player uses live.json for the latest detected status. A live indicator is not a guarantee that the YouTube stream is currently reachable; verify the player on your own site.
