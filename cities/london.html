Sunrise, sunset and daylight in London

London, United Kingdom, lies near 51.5° N. Its location helps explain when local daylight begins and ends, and how that changes throughout the year.

Local time and daylight

London uses GMT in winter and British Summer Time (UTC+1) during the daylight-saving period. Civil clock time is not the same as solar time, so official sunrise and sunset times should be checked with a current astronomical source when precision matters.

How the seasons change the view

Its northern latitude creates long summer evenings and relatively short winter days.
