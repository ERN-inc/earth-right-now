Sunrise, sunset and daylight in Dubai, United Arab Emirates

Dubai, United Arab Emirates lies near 25.2° N. The combination of latitude, Earth's axial tilt and civil clock conventions shapes its sunrise and sunset patterns.

Time zone and solar noon

Gulf Standard Time (UTC+4). Dubai does not use daylight saving time. The city's clock remains on UTC+4 throughout the year. Solar noon need not occur at exactly 12:00 because legal time zones and apparent solar time differ.

Seasons and the changing sky

Dubai has longer daylight near the June solstice and shorter daylight near December, though the difference is less dramatic than at high northern latitudes. The desert climate and atmospheric haze can influence how sunrise looks from the ground, but they do not change the astronomical timing.
