Sunrise, sunset and daylight in Cape Town, South Africa

Cape Town, South Africa lies near 33.9° S. Its latitude and local time conventions help explain why sunrise and sunset look different from other places on Earth.

Local clock and the Sun

South Africa Standard Time (UTC+2). Cape Town uses UTC+2 throughout the year without daylight saving. Its clock time and local solar time are not identical.

Daylight across the seasons

As a southern-hemisphere city, Cape Town has its longest days around December and shortest around June, the reverse of Tokyo and Seoul. The surrounding mountains and coastline can affect where the Sun first becomes visible to an observer.
