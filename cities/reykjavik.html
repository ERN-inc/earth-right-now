Sunrise, sunset and daylight in Reykjavík, Iceland

Reykjavík, Iceland lies near 64.1° N. Its latitude and local time conventions help explain why sunrise and sunset look different from other places on Earth.

Local clock and the Sun

Greenwich Mean Time (UTC+0). Iceland uses UTC+0 year-round, without seasonal clock changes. Reykjavík lies far west of the zero-degree meridian, so solar noon often occurs later than 12:00 on the clock.

Daylight across the seasons

At this high northern latitude, summer brings exceptionally long days and winter brings very short days. Reykjavík is just south of the Arctic Circle, so it does not ordinarily experience full polar night. Long twilight periods make the transition between day and night especially gradual in summer.
