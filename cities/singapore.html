Sunrise, sunset and daylight in Singapore

Singapore lies just north of the equator, at approximately 1.3° N. That location makes its daylight pattern very different from cities at higher latitudes: the length of the day remains close to twelve hours throughout the year.

Why the clock can be surprising

Singapore uses Singapore Standard Time (UTC+8) all year, without daylight saving. Its longitude is about 104° E, while the central meridian of UTC+8 is 120° E. This difference helps explain why solar noon is often noticeably later than 12:00 on the clock, even before accounting for the equation of time.

Seasons without dramatic daylight swings

Unlike London or New York, Singapore does not have long summer evenings or very short winter days. Seasonal changes are more noticeable through rainfall, cloud cover and monsoon patterns than through the length of daylight. Clouds may obscure the visible sunrise even when the astronomical event occurs normally.
