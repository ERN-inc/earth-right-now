Sunrise, sunset and daylight in Mumbai, India

Mumbai, India lies near 19.1° N. Its latitude and local time conventions help explain why sunrise and sunset look different from other places on Earth.

Local clock and the Sun

India Standard Time (UTC+5:30). Mumbai uses one national time zone all year and does not observe daylight saving time. The half-hour offset is a civil-clock convention, not a special feature of solar motion.

Daylight across the seasons

At this tropical northern latitude, daylight is longer around June and shorter around December, though the seasonal contrast is less extreme than in London. Monsoon cloud cover can obscure the visible sunrise without changing the underlying astronomical event.
