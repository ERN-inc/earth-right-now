Sunrise, sunset and daylight in Buenos Aires, Argentina

Buenos Aires, Argentina lies near 34.6° S. The combination of latitude, Earth's axial tilt and civil clock conventions shapes its sunrise and sunset patterns.

Time zone and solar noon

Argentina Time (UTC−3). Buenos Aires currently uses UTC−3 year-round without seasonal clock changes. Solar noon need not occur at exactly 12:00 because legal time zones and apparent solar time differ.

Seasons and the changing sky

The city's longest daylight occurs around the December solstice and its shortest around June, because it lies south of the equator. Its southern latitude creates seasonal daylight contrasts broadly comparable to northern mid-latitude cities, but the seasons are reversed.
