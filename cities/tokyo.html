Sunrise, sunset and daylight in Tokyo

Tokyo, Japan, lies near 35.7° N. Its location helps explain when local daylight begins and ends, and how that changes throughout the year.

Local time and daylight

Japan Standard Time (UTC+9) is used year-round, without daylight-saving time. Civil clock time is not the same as solar time, so official sunrise and sunset times should be checked with a current astronomical source when precision matters.

How the seasons change the view

Around the June solstice, Tokyo has longer daylight hours; around the December solstice, the daylight period is shorter.
