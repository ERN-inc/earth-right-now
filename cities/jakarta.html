Sunrise, sunset and daylight in Jakarta

Jakarta, Indonesia, lies near 6.2° S. Its location helps explain when local daylight begins and ends, and how that changes throughout the year.

Local time and daylight

Western Indonesia Time (UTC+7) applies in Jakarta throughout the year. Civil clock time is not the same as solar time, so official sunrise and sunset times should be checked with a current astronomical source when precision matters.

How the seasons change the view

Jakarta lies close to the equator, so its daylight duration changes much less between seasons than in cities at higher latitudes.
